Privacy Policy

Smart QR Hub
Developer: Casemull
Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Smart QR Hub is a mobile app published by Casemull. This policy describes how the app handles information. It applies only to Smart QR Hub, not to other apps published by Casemull.

Information the App Accesses

Camera: The app uses your device camera only to scan QR codes on your device. Camera frames are processed in real time and are not stored, uploaded, or shared.

Photos: If you choose “Scan from photos,” the app reads the image you select to detect QR codes on-device. Images are not uploaded.

Information Stored on Your Device

Scan history: When you scan a code, the app may save the decoded content locally so you can view it later in History. Scan history stays on your device and is not sent to Casemull servers.

Advertising

Free builds may show a banner on the History tab only, served by Google AdMob after Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) allows ad requests. Ad requests use non-personalized / contextual settings; this app does not implement App Tracking Transparency (ATT). Google's Mobile Ads SDK may still process device and advertising identifiers and related data as described in Google's SDK data disclosure. Ads are never shown on the camera Scan tab. Smart QR Hub Pro removes ads while your subscription entitlement is active. When required, Ad privacy options are available in Settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Optional Pro features are offered as an auto-renewable subscription through the App Store or Google Play. When you purchase or restore:

  • Apple or Google processes payment and provides a purchase receipt.
  • The app uses RevenueCat to validate entitlements so Pro features unlock correctly.
  • Purchase-related data (such as purchase history / entitlement status) may be processed by RevenueCat for app functionality and subscription analytics.
  • We do not use RevenueCat for selling your data.
  • We do not require an account; RevenueCat uses anonymous app identifiers unless you later link an account.

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID or Google Play settings. Restore purchases anytime from Settings.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account or login for local scanning
  • We do not collect scan history on our servers
  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We do not place ads on the camera scanning experience

Data Deletion

You can delete scan history at any time from Settings → Clear history. Deletion is permanent on your device and does not cancel a subscription.

Children

Smart QR Hub is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
