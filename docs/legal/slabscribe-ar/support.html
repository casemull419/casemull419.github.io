SlabScribe AR (Digital Wall Scribing & CNC Countertop Templater)

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the LiDAR scribing?

On Apple Pro devices equipped with LiDAR, SlabScribe AR achieves millimeter-level spatial contour resolution across typical 10-15 foot residential cabinet runs. Always check the printed 50mm calibration box on paper templates before cutting.

Which CNC software formats are supported?

SlabScribe AR exports AutoCAD R14/2000 continuous polyline DXF files compatible with Fusion 360, Vectric VCarve, and industrial CNC waterjets. It also exports standard color-coded SVG files for Shaper Origin handheld routers and laser cutters.

How do I restore my purchases?

In SlabScribe AR, open Settings or the Pro paywall screen and tap "Restore Purchases".
