Privacy Policy

AetherHook: Clockwork Swing
Developer: Casemull
Last updated: 30 October 2026

AetherHook: Clockwork Swing is a single-player steampunk momentum grapple game. It needs no account. This policy explains what stays on your device. Casemull collects no data from the app (App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected).

Data stored on your device

  • Career progress, level best times, cog scrap, unlocked skins, perk drafts, daily clocktower records, ghost replays, and audio/haptic settings are saved locally on your device.
  • An optional local debug log may record gameplay events for QA. It never leaves your device, is not uploaded, and is off by default in release builds.
  • Casemull does not run servers for the game and does not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • There are no ads, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, and no analytics sent off your device.
  • There are no accounts, Game Center, iCloud sync, or online leaderboards.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The game is not directed to children under 13.

In-app purchases

Optional one-time purchases (Master Aeronaut Pass and Workshop Skin Bundle) are processed by Apple through StoreKit on the App Store. Casemull receives no payment details. Entitlements are checked on your device. Both products support Family Sharing under Apple's terms.
