Privacy Policy

CueDungeon: Pocket Ricochet
Developer: Casemull
Last updated: 30 October 2026

CueDungeon: Pocket Ricochet is a single-player tactical billiards roguelite. It needs no account. This policy explains what stays on your device. Casemull collects no data from the app (App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected).

Data stored on your device

  • Career progress, dungeon run records, Golden Chalk, unlocked cues, relic drafts, daily run records, and audio/haptic settings are saved locally on your device.
  • The Cue Transfer Code — a shareable string representing your progress — stays on your device unless you manually copy it to share with another device.
  • An optional local debug log may record gameplay events for QA. It never leaves your device, is not uploaded, and is off by default in release builds.
  • Casemull does not run servers for the game and does not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information.
  • There are no ads, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, and no analytics sent off your device.
  • There are no accounts, Game Center, iCloud sync, or online leaderboards.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The game is not directed to children under 13.

In-app purchases

Optional one-time purchases (Grandmaster Cue Pass, Full Cue Rack, and Supporter Bundle) are processed by Apple through StoreKit on the App Store. Casemull receives no payment details. Entitlements are checked on your device. All products support Family Sharing under Apple's terms.

Apple StoreKit rating prompt

Apple may display the standard App Store rating prompt through StoreKit. Casemull does not receive or track whether you respond to it.
