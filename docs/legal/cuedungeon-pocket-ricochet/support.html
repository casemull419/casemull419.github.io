Common questions

  • Restore purchases: tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the paywall while signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Ask to Buy / pending purchases: if a purchase is waiting on Family Sharing Ask to Buy approval, it stays pending until approved or declined. Entitlements unlock only after Apple confirms the transaction; tap Restore Purchases after approval if the unlock does not appear.
  • Refunds: request refunds through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Casemull cannot issue App Store refunds directly.
  • Cue Transfer Code: the Cue Transfer Code is a shareable string representing your progress. It stays on your device unless you manually copy it. Tap the CUE TRANSFER CODE button on the main menu (also offered after clearing room 8) to generate and copy the code, then paste it into the same dialog on the target device to restore your progress.
  • Lost progress: progress is saved on your device only, so uninstalling the app deletes it. Use the Cue Transfer Code to back up your progress before uninstalling.
