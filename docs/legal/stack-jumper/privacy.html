Privacy Policy

Stack Jumper

Effective date and last updated: 2026-11-08

About This Policy

Stack Jumper is published by Casemull. The core game works offline and does not require an account. This policy explains local game data, Google AdMob advertising, and purchases processed by Apple.

Data Stored on Your Device

Scores, saved runs, settings, tutorial progress, coins, missions, cosmetics, daily progress, local leaderboards, gameplay statistics, and a cached purchase entitlement are stored on your device. Casemull does not receive these local saves.

Gameplay analytics are local-only. We do not send gameplay analytics to a developer analytics service. This is separate from the advertising measurement and diagnostics collected by Google AdMob below.

Advertising and Data Shared with Google AdMob

Stack Jumper shows Google AdMob ads, provided by Google LLC. Optional rewarded ads offer a continue or coin doubler after game over when you choose Watch Ad. Occasional interstitials may appear when leaving a completed run's summary; they do not interrupt an active climb, pause, resume, or Save & Exit. Ads require connectivity and may be unavailable.

To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and diagnose problems, the AdMob SDK may collect and share the following with Google:

  • Device identifiers and the advertising identifier (IDFA), subject to the consent and tracking choices below.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Ad impressions, ad interactions, and app interaction data used for advertising measurement.
  • Device and app information, crash reports, diagnostics, and performance data.

Google processes advertising data under its own policies. See Google's partner data policy. Local-only gameplay analytics do not mean that the advertising SDK collects no data.

Consent and Tracking Choices

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) handles ad consent before ads load and presents a consent form where required. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is resolved after the UMP flow. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only after the UMP consent flow permits the relevant use and you grant ATT tracking permission. Where consent is required, that consent must also be granted.

If you deny ATT permission, IDFA is not shared, but contextual ads may still appear if UMP permits ad requests. If consent does not permit ad requests, ads do not load. The core game remains playable when ads are unavailable.

Open Privacy Policy on the main menu and choose Privacy Options when offered by UMP to review or withdraw ad consent. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Remove Ads and Apple Purchases

Remove Ads is an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Remove Ads removes interstitials; optional rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them. It is not a subscription and is not required to play.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Apple handles payment details; Casemull does not receive your card or billing details. The app receives purchase verification and entitlement information and caches the entitlement on your device.

Use Restore Purchases on the main menu with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase to restore an eligible Remove Ads purchase without buying it again. Purchases and restoration require a connection to Apple. Removing local data does not erase Apple's purchase records.

Your Data and Choices

You can reset your best score in the menu or uninstall the app to remove its local save. Uninstalling does not delete data already processed by Google or Apple; their policies and account controls govern that data. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use them to respond.

Sound and vibration are optional settings. Vibration is used for haptic feedback.

Children and Age

Stack Jumper is a general-audience game. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use, including advertising choices and purchases. Advertising data may be processed as described in this policy; an age rating does not mean no data is collected. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through the app.
