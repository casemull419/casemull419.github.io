Common questions

  • Restore purchases: tap RESTORE PURCHASES on the main menu while signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Ad and tracking choices: tap PRIVACY CHOICES on the main menu (shown where a consent choice applies), or change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Lost progress: progress is saved on your device only, so uninstalling the app deletes it.
