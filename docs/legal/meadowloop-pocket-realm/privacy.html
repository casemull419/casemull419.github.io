Privacy Policy

MeadowLoop: Pocket Realm
Developer: Casemull
Last updated: 29 October 2026

MeadowLoop: Pocket Realm is a hexagonal landscape puzzle game. It needs no account. This policy explains what stays on your device and what our advertising partner may collect.

Data stored on your device

  • Island snapshots, Harmony scores, Daily Harmony records, Realm Codex unlocks and audio, haptics and lighting preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • Realm Codes and Postcards are created on your device and are only shared if you choose to share them.
  • Casemull does not run servers for the game and does not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Rewarded ads are optional and appear only when you tap a button marked "▶ Ad" or "Watch Ad". Interstitial ads may appear on the realm-complete screen, at most once every 5 minutes, and never once you have made any purchase.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers (the iOS advertising identifier only if you allow tracking);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared, though ads may still appear. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a Google consent message asks for your choices before ads load. Tap PRIVACY CHOICES on the main menu to review or withdraw consent at any time.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Casemull receives no payment details. Your entitlement is checked on your device through StoreKit.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Casemull runs no analytics of its own and uses no accounts, Game Center or iCloud.
  • The game is not directed to children under 13.
