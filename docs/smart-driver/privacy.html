Overview

Smart Driver is a casual puzzle game. Game progress is saved locally on your device.

Advertising

Smart Driver may show optional advertisements from Google AdMob after completed levels or when you choose a rewarded bonus. Google may process device advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data according to Google's advertising policies. Smart Driver does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use IDFA for cross-app tracking.

Analytics

Smart Driver does not enable third-party analytics in this build.

Purchases

No in-app purchases are active in this build.
