Privacy Policy

SlabScribe AR
Developer: Casemull Studio
Last updated: October 2026

Overview

SlabScribe AR is a digital wall scribing and CNC countertop templater app published by Casemull. This policy describes how the app handles sensor information and measurements on your device. We are committed to complete on-device privacy.

Information the App Accesses

Camera & LiDAR Depth Sensor: The app uses your device camera and LiDAR sensor strictly to capture 3D spatial geometry and raycast base-cabinet reference planes. Depth points are processed in real-time memory to calculate 2D spline contours. Camera frames and depth point clouds are never transmitted, uploaded, or shared.

Information Stored on Your Device

Job Templates & Scribe Projects: When you save a countertop template, measurement project, or client note, the data is saved exclusively on your local device. This data is not sent to Casemull or third-party servers.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require account registration or cloud sign-in.
  • We do not collect personal information, jobsite measurements, or CAD drawings on our servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share user data.
  • We do not embed third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.
